01 · What TASA11 is

An ecosystem, not a results board.

TASA11 sits between the horse and the decision. It gathers what the animal shows, what the season records, what the camera sees, and what experienced people know — then returns that as intelligence a horseman can use.

It is Saudi-first and globally connected: rooted in the Kingdom’s racing life, and built to converse with markets that have refined this craft for generations.

The work is not to replace the trainer, the owner, the veterinarian, the bloodstock agent, or the commentator. It is to give them a clearer field of options, then leave the call where it belongs — with the human.

  1. Equine Intelligence ecosystem TASA11, Red Team X, specialist agents, computer vision, and the Smart Digital Stable are one system around the horse — not a stack of disconnected tools.
  2. Saudi-first, globally connected The proving ground is the Saudi racing season. The ambition is a conversation with established racing and bloodstock worlds, not a claim to stand in for them.
  3. Decisions stay human Intelligence prepares the ground. People who live with horses still make the decision.

02 · Intelligence stack

What the ecosystem holds together.

Seven layers, one direction of travel: from the living horse toward a decision a person can stand behind.

01

Horse Data

The living record of the animal — identity, work, condition, and the quiet facts that accumulate around a horse over time.

02

Expert Knowledge

Judgement from people who have stood in the yard, the vet box, the sales ring, and the commentary box. Knowledge the industry already has — held so it can be used again.

03

Racing Data

The public and private grammar of the sport: form, conditions, meetings, and the outcomes that teach the next reading.

04

Video / Computer Vision

What the eye sees at work and at the races, made reviewable. Movement, pattern, and replay as evidence — not as spectacle.

05

Red Team X

The intelligence engine and expert-learning layer. It tests what the data suggests against what experienced people know, then holds both to the same standard.

06

Specialist Agents

Focused companions for prediction, commentary, the stable, and later the sales ring — each one narrow in task, each one answerable to the same evidence.

07

Decision Support

The last layer is not a verdict. It is a set of options, with the reasoning visible, so a horseman can decide.

05 · Intelligence engine

Red
Team X

The layer that learns from experts, then tests what it learned.

Red Team X is the intelligence engine behind TASA11. It is not a dashboard and it is not a black box pitched as magic. It is the place where horse data, racing evidence, vision, and specialist judgement are held to account.

Experts teach it. It asks back. What survives that exchange becomes options the rest of the ecosystem can offer — to a trainer, an owner, a commentator, a bloodstock desk, or a racing organisation.

The architecture stays in the engine room. What matters in the yard is simpler: better questions, clearer options, and a human still making the decision.

TASA11 does not arrive to replace Newmarket, Kentucky, Ireland, Japan, France, Australia, or any other home of this craft. Those places hold expertise the Kingdom can learn from — and they, in turn, can meet a Saudi-born intelligence layer that takes the horse as seriously as they do.

The posture is study and conversation: to listen to how established racing and bloodstock markets work, then to build an ecosystem that can stand in that company without borrowing their names as trophies.
